Wishlytic The revenue waterfall

Valve's cut is tiered, not a flat 30%

Discount, regional pricing and VAT, refunds, and only then a 30/25/20 royalty on adjusted gross — every deduction between the sticker price and the developer's bank account, named and sized.

Taking a flat 30% off the list price instead inflates the answer by roughly 60%.
